About me

Welcome to my website! I am a second-year Ph.D. student studying chemical engineering at MIT. I am generally interested in developing and applying GPU-based nonlinear programming techniques for dynamic optimization. My development work is focused on parametric sensitivity-based adaptive mesh relocation on GPU and GPU-based batched interior-point methods for nonlinear programs. My applied work is about parameter estimation and optimal control for systems biology and energy systems. For fun, I like to go to the gym, run along the Charles River, and play video games.
